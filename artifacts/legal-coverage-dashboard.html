מפת דרכים לפי כיסוי (Roadmap)

Sprint 1

Legislation — realize the open corpus

Highest ROI: VERIFIED open Knesset OData, no auth, one collector, 0% ingested today.

  • Build the Knesset OData collector (KNS_IsraelLaw, KNS_Bill, KNS_DocumentIsraelLaw, committees).
  • Persist through the fail-closed pipeline (UNPUBLISHED, dedup, restriction-skip).

↳ Primary-law grounding for every downstream legal-reasoning feature.

Sprint 2

Case-law — open tribunals now, official feed next

Biggest value gap. Capture what is open immediately; start the institutional path for core courts.

  • Ingest ararim + mishmoret (2 OPEN full-document tribunal sets).
  • Open an official-feed / FOI track for Supreme/District/Magistrate/Labor court data (NO scraping of anti-bot systems).

↳ First real judgment corpus; groundwork for labor-court coverage (the firm's core).

Sprint 3

Registries — cheap structured breadth

Lowest difficulty, already OPEN; one CKAN adapter serves all 18 ready datasets.

  • Configure the CKAN collector for companies / nonprofits / IP / inheritance registries.
  • Honor cc-by attribution obligations on the flagged datasets.

↳ Diligence & entity-resolution layer.

Sprint 4

Reference layer — Kol Zchut

Open CC-BY-SA API, easy, high client-facing value as a rights-reference layer.

  • Ingest Kol Zchut via MediaWiki API with attribution.
  • Keep it labelled secondary/reference, never as primary law.

↳ Client-facing rights explanations.

Sprint 5

Regulatory & Government — audit before build

Estimated only; must be audited per-regulator. gov.il stays WAF-blocked → official feed only.

  • Per-regulator live audit (ISA/BoI/CMA/Tax/Privacy) from an unblocked network.
  • Pursue an official government-resolutions feed; do not scrape the WAF.

↳ Regulatory & administrative coverage once lawful routes are confirmed.

ההמלצה האסטרטגית הבאה

בנה תחילה את Collector החקיקה של הכנסת (Knesset OData) — 48 ישויות, פתוח, ללא הזדהות, וכרגע 0% ממומש. זהו ה-ROI הגבוה ביותר על הלוח והוא פותח את כל שכבות ההיגיון המשפטי שמעליו. במקביל: לכוד את 2 מאגרי הטריבונלים הפתוחים, ופתח מסלול מוסדי (בקשת מידע / הסכם נתונים) לפסיקת הערכאות הליבתיות — מה שגריפה חוקית לעולם לא תשיג.
