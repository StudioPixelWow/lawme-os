# National Legal Knowledge Coverage Map — 2026-08-06

**Method: pure synthesis.** This map was produced with **no** network calls, no
database changes, no discovery, no audit, no qualification, and no collectors.
It re-reads the artifacts already produced by earlier lawful passes — the
platform registry, the data.gov.il dataset qualification, and the discovery
catalog — and organizes them into a taxonomy of Israeli legal knowledge with an
honest coverage score per domain. Generated by
`tools/legal-source-audit/coverage-map.mjs` →
`artifacts/legal-coverage.json`.

## The one distinction that governs everything

There are two very different questions, and this document keeps them apart at
all times:

- **Available coverage** — how much of a legal domain is *lawfully reachable
  today* (open license, no auth, no anti-bot barrier).
- **Realized coverage** — how much is *actually ingested* into LawME.

**Realized coverage is currently 0 across every domain**, because no collector
has run. Every "coverage" figure below that is not explicitly labelled
*realized* is an *availability* figure — a map of what we could lawfully build,
not a corpus we already hold. Reporting these as the same number would be the
single most misleading thing this analysis could do, so it never does.

National availability (value-weighted): **50 / 100**.
National realized: **0 / 100**.

## Taxonomy — 7 domains of Israeli legal knowledge

1. **פסיקה — Case Law / Judiciary.** Supreme, District, Magistrate, Labor
   courts; specialized tribunals; Rabbinical / Sharia / Military courts.
2. **חקיקה — Legislation.** Primary laws, bills, secondary legislation
   (regulations), Knesset committee documents, the Reshumot gazette.
3. **מרשמים — Registries.** Companies, partnerships, nonprofits, patents,
   trademarks, inheritance & endowments.
4. **רגולציה — Regulatory.** Decisions and directives of ISA, Bank of Israel,
   CMA, Competition Authority, Privacy Authority, Tax rulings, Regulation DB.
5. **מנהלי — Government / Administrative.** Government resolutions, ministry
   decisions, administrative orders across gov.il.
6. **אקדמי/משני — Academic & Secondary.** Law reviews, theses, commentary, and
   public rights-reference material (Kol Zchut).
7. **בינלאומי/משווה — International & Comparative.** Treaties, foreign and
   comparative law, international rulings.

### What each score is built on

**Legislation (available 94, VERIFIED).** The Knesset OData ParliamentInfo API
was verified this program: 48 entity sets including `KNS_IsraelLaw`,
`KNS_Bill`, and `KNS_DocumentIsraelLaw` — full law and bill texts, open, no
authentication. The corpus is essentially fully reachable; it is simply not yet
ingested. This is the highest-ROI position on the board.

**Registries (available 85, VERIFIED).** The best-covered domain by open
license count: companies (`ica_companies`, `ica-changes`, `ica_partnerships`),
nonprofits (`moj-amutot`), patents and trademarks (`mamtziim_patents`,
`trademarks_nice`, `simaneymisahr`), inheritance (`yerusha`, `hekdeshot`). These
are structured registry *records*, not full legal documents — powerful for
diligence and entity resolution, but not primary-law research. One CKAN adapter
serves all 18 ready datasets.

**Case Law (available 31, VERIFIED — and this is the headline gap).** This is
the highest-value domain (legal 5, business 5 — a labor-law firm lives on
judgments) and the least reachable. The only open full-text case law we can hold
today is **two tribunal decision sets** — `ararim` (בתי הדין לעררים) and
`mishmoret` (בתי הדין למשמורת). Core court judgments — Supreme, District,
Magistrate, and critically **Labor** — sit behind net-hamishpat's anti-bot
posture and have **no lawful bulk route**. The `judgments` CKAN dataset is
metadata-plus-summary only, not full text. Closing this gap requires an
**official court data feed** pursued institutionally (FOI / data agreement), not
scraping.

**Regulatory & Government (available 25, ESTIMATED).** Regulation *metadata* is
open (`regulationdatabase`), but the regulators' decision endpoints are
unaudited estimates, and gov.il's government-resolutions corpus is behind a WAF
(server-side 403). Both need lawful routes confirmed before any build.

**Academic (available 55, ESTIMATED).** Split personality: Kol Zchut is VERIFIED
open (MediaWiki API, 7,347 articles, CC-BY-SA) and makes an excellent
client-facing *rights-reference* layer — but it is secondary, not primary law.
University law-review / thesis repositories (OAI-PMH) are entirely unaudited, so
that half of the domain is genuinely UNKNOWN.

**International (available 5, UNKNOWN).** No open Israeli machine source was
identified this program; lowest priority for the firm's practice.

## Platform → domain grounding

Only VERIFIED, open platforms anchor a domain's availability score:

- **Knesset OData** → Legislation (48 entity sets, open, no auth).
- **data.gov.il (CKAN)** → Registries + the 2 open Case-Law tribunal sets.
- **Kol Zchut (MediaWiki)** → Academic/Secondary reference layer.

Everything else (gov.il WAF, net-hamishpat anti-bot, university OAI-PMH, NLI,
regulator endpoints) is ESTIMATED or blocked and is scored accordingly — never
credited as coverage it hasn't earned.

## Honesty ledger

- Realized coverage is 0 everywhere; availability ≠ possession.
- Confidence is VERIFIED only where a real API response was captured this
  program (Legislation, Registries, Kol Zchut, the 2 tribunal sets). Everything
  else is ESTIMATED or UNKNOWN and labelled as such.
- Case-law core courts are reported as **not covered and not lawfully
  bulk-reachable** — no pretense of a scraping route around anti-bot systems.
- All figures trace to `artifacts/legal-coverage.json`, itself derived only from
  previously-collected artifacts.
