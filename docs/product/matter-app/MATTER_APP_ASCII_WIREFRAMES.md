# Matter App — ASCII Wireframes (Epic 5.1)

RTL-aware wireframes at ~1440px. **RTL note:** the navy SideRail is at the
**start/right** edge; the utility/Context Dock is at the **end/left** edge; reading
flows right→left. In ASCII (inherently LTR) the right-most column is the SideRail to
preserve the true RTL placement. Example content uses the real fixture: *"פיטורי
עובדת בהריון — כהן נ׳ טק-ליין"* (pregnancy_dismissal), currently at the
fact-confirmation stage, missing a supporting affidavit, hearing in 4 days.

Legend: `◆` missing-item diamond · `◉` current/now node (breathes gold) · `●` done ·
`○` future (dashed) · `⚑` דינו seal · `▲` risk pulse · `⌘K` command bar.

deadline → spine → lenses; the one action is a sticky button; דינו is a bottom sheet.
